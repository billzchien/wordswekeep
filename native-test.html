Native script test

Each case opens the archive straight in the notes of a made-up quote (the other scripts: public-domain lines). Every script should be set in its Noto, sized to sit with the English beside it: Chinese, Japanese, Korean in Noto Sans; Russian and Greek in Noto Serif (italic in a book title, like English); Arabic, Hebrew, Thai, Hindi in their Noto Sans, upright. Arabic and Hebrew read right to left, each sentence keeping its own full stop.

Look at

  • Words from: the author's name, and the source name under it
  • Kept by: the name
  • The context and the personal note
  • An underlined word in the quote: its explanation
  • The language button (中 日 한): the quote in its original language
